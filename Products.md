## 3. Customer Enquiry and Quotation Guidance

Use the product details in this document when responding to customer enquiries.

1. Identify the product the customer needs.
2. Confirm the required size and design.
3. Confirm the quantity and compare it with the product MOQ.
4. Confirm the requested background colour, print colours and finishing.
5. Share the stated lead time. For the label products above, explain that the lead time begins after receipt of the advance payment.
6. Use the relevant product's stated pricing information. Do not assume that one product's rate applies to another.
7. Refer non-standard background colours and colour requests that exceed the stated limits for review.
8. Explain that pricing is negotiable for quantities above the MOQ.
9. For MOQ quantities, where applicable, encourage a simpler single-colour design as a cost-conscious option.

## 4. Important Handling Notes

- Do not promise a customised colour, additional print colour, special design or non-standard requirement until it has been reviewed.
- Do not provide a final quotation if required details such as size or design are missing.
- Keep each product's MOQ, lead time, pricing and finishing options separate.
- All numeric values, prices, rates, percentages, dimensions, quantities and lead times from the supplied notes have been retained.
